What even is this?

LGBTQ++ stands for Language-General Benchmarking of Task Quality. The "language-general" part is aspirational: the ++ is C++, and C++ is the only language we benchmark so far.

We are a very serious organization with one goal: to find out whose C++ solution is the fastest. Everyone who solves the task throws their program in the ring, and our arena.sh turns the test run into a live game show with a leaderboard, commentary and a podium.

Correct answers earn points. Speed earns more points. A streak earns bragging rights. Wrong answers earn the cat's disappointment.
